Membership Eligibility Requirements

In order to be eligible for the SFBAFCC membership, candidates must:

  1. Be film critics living in the Greater Bay Area (i.e., San Francisco, the East Bay, Marin County, Sacramento area, and the South Bay encompassing San Jose) working regularly for at least two years in a professional journalistic medium, including print, TV, radio, and the internet, but not college or university media.
  2. Be published or broadcast in the medium at least 50 times annually, and have been for at least two full years prior to applying for membership.
  3. Not be in the paid employ of filmmakers, distributors, exhibitors, publicity firms, or advertising agencies.
  4. See the majority of major releases in any given year of membership in the course of their work as critics.
  5. Agree during their first year of membership to a minimum of 10 hours' attendance at SFBAFCC functions beyond the mandatory annual awards meeting.
